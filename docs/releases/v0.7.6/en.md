# Arisa32 v0.7.6

## Changes and fixes

### Dal Segno (MangaGamer)

- In the opening, the lens flare sweeps across the sky before the first line of dialogue. The airport-to-beach transition and CG gallery wipes now follow the direction and timing in the game scripts.
- White petals spin across the screen during scene changes, including the walk from the beach to the dorms. The smaller petals stay visible until their animation finishes.
- Scenario Replay shows the title of the scene you hover over or press. Gallery thumbnails and arrows disappear when replay starts, so a single tap on the date card can advance the story.
- Waves and other looping sounds stop when you end a replay or return to the title screen. Loading a save restores the sound effects recorded in that slot.

### D.C. II P.C. (Da Capo II Plus Communication)

- Each December 20–23 alarm choice now leads to the corresponding morning scene. Back opens the sidebar, and closing it returns you to the alarm choices. The alarm panel stays intact after you release a button.

### D.C. II Dearest Marriage

- The Start menu now lets the “Graduation Days”, “Married Life”, and “Dear to” voice lines finish before the screen fades. Opening movies also wait for the voice line to finish.
